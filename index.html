FIELD NOTES: BUILDING AN OSINT PRACTICE

1

START WITH DAILY REPS

5-10 minute drills you can do every day. Think warm-ups: reverse image search, WHOIS lookup, quick geolocation. Build the habit first.

2

WEEKLY DEEP DIVES

Set aside 30-60 minutes for structured investigations. Pick a random person/company, build a full profile. Document your process.

3

MONTHLY SKILL AUDITS

Test new tools, review techniques you've neglected, update your toolkit. The OSINT landscape changes fast.

4

TRACK YOUR STREAKS

Consistency beats intensity. A 7-day streak of 10-minute drills builds more skill than sporadic 3-hour sessions.

5

DOCUMENT EVERYTHING

Keep a logbook (digital or physical). What worked? What failed? Which source was most reliable? Review quarterly.

6

JOIN A COMMUNITY

Trace Labs CTFs, OSINT Curious Discord, Bellingcat's workshops. Learning alone is slow. Learning together is exponential.
